# Funnel Framework

| Stage | Definition | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| Visit | Customer reaches website | Total Visitors |
| Product View | Customer views a product | Product View Rate |
| Add to Cart | Purchase intent | Add-to-Cart Rate |
| Checkout | Checkout begins | Checkout From Cart Rate |
| Purchase | Transaction completed | Conversion Rate |
| Retention | Customer remains after purchase | Retention Rate |

Analyze both overall visitor-to-purchase conversion and stage-to-stage conversion to locate bottlenecks.
