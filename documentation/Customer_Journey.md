# Customer Journey Map

```text
Acquisition → Website Visit → Product View → Add to Cart → Checkout → Purchase → Retention
```

## Business Questions
- Acquisition: which channels and campaigns attract customers?
- Visit: do visitors engage or bounce?
- Product view: is the offer compelling?
- Cart: is there purchase intent?
- Checkout: where is friction occurring?
- Purchase: which behaviors produce revenue?
- Retention: which customers remain engaged?
