# Dashboard Build Specification

## Page: Executive Customer Journey Overview

### Top
Five KPI cards: Total Visitors, Leads Generated, Conversion Rate, Retention Rate, Total Revenue.

### Middle
- Funnel: Visit → Product View → Add to Cart → Checkout → Purchase
- Customer Journey flow
- Acquisition Channel conversion performance

### Bottom
- Customer segment/region performance
- Retention analysis
- Funnel drop-off analysis
- Recommendations/insight panel

### Right Sidebar
Slicers: date, acquisition channel, customer segment, region, campaign name, age group.

## Design
16:9 desktop canvas, corporate product analytics theme, light neutral background, dark typography, one consistent accent family, generous whitespace, aligned visuals, concise titles, no 3D charts.
