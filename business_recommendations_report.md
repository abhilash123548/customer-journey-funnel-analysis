# Business Recommendations Report

## Executive Summary
The project evaluates the customer journey from acquisition to retention. The objective is to identify conversion bottlenecks, compare acquisition sources, and prioritize growth actions.

## Recommended Actions
1. Prioritize the largest stage-to-stage funnel drop-off rather than optimizing traffic volume alone.
2. Scale channels that combine strong conversion with meaningful revenue.
3. Improve low-converting campaigns through landing-page, offer, message, and checkout testing.
4. Build retention journeys for customers with lower feedback or churn signals.
5. Segment performance by region, customer segment, and age group before reallocating budget.

## Measurement Plan
Track conversion rate, add-to-cart rate, checkout rate, revenue per visitor, average purchase value, retention rate, bounce rate, and feedback score weekly.

## CAC Limitation
CAC requires marketing spend. This dataset does not contain campaign spend, so CAC should be added before making budget-allocation decisions.
